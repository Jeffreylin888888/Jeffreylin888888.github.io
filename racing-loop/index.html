3 · The intervention layer keeps the opponent's driving legal

Some Scenic knob settings make the opponent break the racing rules, for example moving over onto ART when ART is already alongside. The intervention layer is a Python script that sits between the Scenic knobs and the simulator and handles each IAC passing rule. Every 5 milliseconds it checks both cars against every rule. When a rule would be broken, it changes the opponent's motion just enough to stay legal. Otherwise it leaves the opponent exactly as the Scenic knobs made it.
